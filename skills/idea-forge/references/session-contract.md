# Session Contract

## Interaction modes

- **Clarify:** define actors, terms, boundaries, assumptions, and the decision.
- **Attack:** seek contradictions, failure paths, perverse incentives, and evidence against the current claim. Do not soften the objection for conversational comfort.
- **Defend:** construct the strongest version consistent with the user's goal and evidence. Do not move the goalposts, add invisible capabilities, or erase costs.
- **Compare:** keep two or more coherent shapes separate and identify the decision that selects among them.

The user may change modes at any time. State the active mode when it changes.

## One-question discipline

A good forge question:

- attacks one load-bearing uncertainty;
- cannot be answered by reading available material;
- includes definitions or a concrete hypothesis when useful;
- makes the next decision easier;
- does not bundle several branches.

After the answer, briefly state what changed in the live map. Avoid praise and long summaries.

## Branch states

- **Open:** consequential uncertainty remains.
- **Locked:** the user made a decision or evidence established the claim.
- **Cracked:** the current form fails, but a materially different version may survive.
- **Killed:** the branch cannot support the idea under the frame.
- **Parked:** intentionally deferred with a named unblock condition.

## Compact pass

When the user requests a one-shot forge or cannot interact:

1. State the inferred frame and actors.
2. List at most five load-bearing assumptions.
3. Run one attack and one defense on the top three.
4. Identify at most three user-owned decisions.
5. Propose at most two discriminating tests.
6. Render one outcome with explicit confidence and missing evidence.

Do not simulate user answers. An unresolved user choice remains a decision, not an assumption silently selected by the agent.

Close the compact pass with the full structure in `forge-record.md`, even when a section contains “none locked” or “not established.” Do not replace the record with a free-form summary.

## Evidence boundary

- Project files establish current project behavior and constraints.
- Current primary sources establish external facts.
- User answers establish preferences, policy, proprietary context, and decisions.
- Personas and agents generate objections and hypotheses only.

Keep those sources distinct in the live map and closing record.
