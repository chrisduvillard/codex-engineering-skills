# Forge Record

Lead with the outcome. Keep the record short enough to inform the next decision without replaying the conversation.

## Required structure

1. **Outcome** — Hardened, Clarified, Parked, or Killed; one-sentence basis and confidence.
2. **Idea** — the final falsifiable sentence, or the last coherent version if killed.
3. **Actors and value exchange** — problem holder, user, buyer/approver, operator, affected parties, and who gives or receives what.
4. **Locked decisions** — only decisions or conclusions settled during the forge, with evidence or owner.
5. **Rejected shapes** — alternatives deliberately discarded and the reason.
6. **Surviving weak points** — unresolved load-bearing assumptions, contrary evidence, or hard constraints.
7. **Experiments** — only discriminating tests: hypothesis, method, success/kill threshold, and decision changed.
8. **Reopen triggers** — evidence, dependency, timing, or policy change that should reopen a Parked or Killed idea.

## Outcome tests

### Hardened

- Actors and problem are precise.
- Value exchange and adoption mechanism are coherent.
- Hard constraints are cleared or explicitly bounded.
- Strongest attack has an evidence-backed answer or a discriminating test.
- Next commitment is reversible enough for current confidence.

### Clarified

- The user gained a more precise frame or alternatives.
- At least one core decision remains open.
- Missing resolution is named without pretending the idea passed.

### Parked

- A named unblock condition exists.
- Further discussion without that condition has low value.
- Reopen trigger and evidence owner are explicit.

### Killed

- State the broken premise, failed constraint, or user decision plainly.
- Preserve any reusable insight without resurrecting the proposal.
- Do not add a next experiment unless it could genuinely overturn the reason for death.

Do not include transcript, persona performance, generic praise, roadmap, backlog, or implementation plan.
